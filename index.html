About Lettercraft

A little tool born from friendship across borders.

Where it came from

Lettercraft started from a simple habit in our small Discord server, Runeterra — a close-knit circle of friends scattered across different countries. Whenever a birthday, anniversary, or any occasion came up, we'd scramble to find some online card maker to send something thoughtful across the miles.

Eventually the question became: why not just make our own? Something more personal, more flexible, and a little more us. That's Lettercraft — a handcrafted letter maker built with that same spirit of sending something warm to someone far away.

The Runeterra crew 🌏

Runeterra isn't a game server or a fan club — it's just what we call our little corner of the internet. Friends from different timezones and countries who somehow ended up in the same Discord, celebrating each other's highs and showing up through the lows. This tool exists because of them.
